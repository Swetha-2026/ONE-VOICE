# ONE VOICE

### Every Voice Tells a Story. Together, We Turn Stories into Action.

ONE VOICE is a community-focused platform that helps people report local problems and turns individual complaints into meaningful community action.

Instead of treating every complaint as a separate issue, ONE VOICE connects similar reports, identifies common problems, highlights priority areas, and helps communities track action.

---

## 🌍 Problem

Many local problems are reported repeatedly but remain disconnected.

Examples include:

* Water shortages
* Damaged roads
* Broken streetlights
* Garbage and sanitation issues
* Waterlogging
* Local safety concerns
* Poor public facilities

People may report the same issue multiple times without knowing that others are facing the same problem.

**ONE VOICE connects these individual voices into a collective view of community needs.**

---

## 💡 Our Solution

ONE VOICE follows a simple flow:

**Report → Understand → Group → Prioritize → Alert → Act → Track**

A citizen can submit an issue using:

* 📝 Text
* 🎤 Voice
* 📷 Image
* 📍 Location

The platform then organizes the information so that similar reports can be connected and community-level problems can be identified.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📝 Multi-Modal Reporting

Users can report community issues through text, voice, images and location.

### 🧩 Problem Clustering

Similar reports can be grouped together to identify one underlying community issue.

### 📊 Community Priority

Issues can be organized based on severity, frequency and community impact.

### 🚨 Early Alerts

Repeated or increasing reports can help identify emerging community problems.

### 🗺️ Interactive Map

Reported issues can be viewed geographically to understand where problems are concentrated.

### 👥 Community

Users can view and understand issues reported by other people in their community.

### 📋 My Reports

Users can track the issues they have personally reported.

### 🔔 Alerts

Important updates and emerging issues can be communicated through the alert system.

### ✅ Action Tracker

Community issues can be followed from reporting to action and resolution.

### 🧩 Community Puzzle

An interactive way of connecting related community problems and understanding the bigger picture.

### 👤 Profile

Users can manage their profile and access their activity.

### 🌐 Language Support

The interface is designed to support multiple languages for wider accessibility.
